A make.fun one-liner entry: the funniest possible last words a phone would say when its battery hits 1 percent.

The joke lands by giving the phone a specific, bitter voice: it counts the 47 open apps, calls out max brightness, names the three-hour doomscrolling session, and delivers the killer detail — the charger is in another room. The sign-off ("magnificent idiot," "we both knew this day would come") reframes the owner's negligence as a shared tragicomedy. It's not a generic farewell; it's a character study in 180 characters.
